# Data availability

The study dataset is **not included** in this release.

The omitted material includes raw and processed EEG, sEMG, and IMU signals; the source clinical manifest; clinician-authored free text; and direct or linkable patient identifiers. These materials are governed by ethics approval, participant consent, institutional policy, and data-use agreements and therefore cannot be redistributed as part of a public code package.

This directory is intentionally a documentation-only placeholder. Do not place confidential study data here before sharing or archiving this release.

Researchers seeking access must contact the project owner and obtain the required institutional and ethics authorization. Access is not implied by availability of the code, checkpoints, aggregate metrics, or de-identified evaluation artifacts.

## 数据说明

本发布包不包含原始或预处理后的 EEG、sEMG、IMU 信号，不包含原始临床标签清单、医生自由文本以及任何可直接或间接识别受试者的信息。上述资料受伦理审批、知情同意、机构管理要求和数据使用协议约束，不能随公开代码包发布。

本目录仅保留这份说明文件。公开分享本项目时，请勿把服务器上的真实研究数据复制到此目录。确需使用数据时，应通过项目负责人和所属机构完成正式申请及授权。
